About

Click here to view full image.

Check checkboxes in a grid to find items, and check all of them to win.
Each cell is a location, and can't be reached until its column and row are unlocked.
The center row and column, and thus the center-most cell, are unlocked by default.


Data

Data is accurate for the latest APWorld version (0.1.0).

Feature Description
Locations Depending on settings, either 9, 25, 49, or 81 locations.
Items Four to sixteen unique progression items (Column and Row Unlocks), one useful item (Free Scouting), one trap item (Death Trap), and one filler item. Free Scouting and Trap count depend on settings.
Goal Check every location.
Traps Contains a single Trap item, the Death Trap.
Protocols Supports the Death Link protocol, with support for Trap Link and Scout Link being planned for future updates.
Options Eight options to control grid size, item rate, Death Link, and the ability to kill-bind.
